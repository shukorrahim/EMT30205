L2.1The basic instructions

In one sentenceA mnemonic is a short word for each part of the ladder: LD starts a line, AND joins in series, OR joins in parallel, NOT flips a contact, OUT drives an output, and END finishes the program.

Pick an instruction. Tap the contacts in the ladder to switch the inputs ON and OFF, and tap a row of the mnemonic table to see which contact it is.

L2.2Normally open vs normally closed

In one sentenceIn a PLC you choose NO or NC in the program, not in the wiring: the same input can open one contact and close another.

Press and hold the push button. Watch its two contacts move inside, then see what the same input 00000 does to three different rungs.

Do not mix up "closed" and "ON". A normal switch must be closed to light a lamp. In a PLC, an NC contact in the program is "closed" while the input is OFF, because the PLC can flip the meaning of a switch in software, with no rewiring.

L2.3The self-holding circuit

In one sentenceA self-holding (latch) circuit keeps a motor running after the START button is released, because the motor's own contact takes over from START.
Analogy: holding a door open. START is your hand pushing the door open. The MOTOR contact is a door stopper you slide in while the door is open. When you let go, the stopper keeps it open. STOP kicks the stopper away and the door closes.
MOTOR 01000: OFF

L2.4Internal relays

In one sentenceAn internal relay is a memory bit that behaves like a relay inside the PLC: you can switch it ON with one rung and use its contact in another, with no real wire.
Analogy: a sticky note. Rung 0 works out a result and writes it on a sticky note (bit 20000). Rung 1 reads the sticky note and uses it together with another switch. Internal relays use addresses 20000 to 23115 (the work area you met in Lab 1).
Internal relay 20000 (memory only, no real wire)

Tap the contacts 00001 to 00004 to switch the inputs. Try to light output 01000.

L2.5AND LD and OR LD: joining blocks

In one sentenceWhen a rung is made of blocks, each block starts with its own LD, and AND LD or OR LD joins the blocks together: in series (AND LD) or in parallel (OR LD).

Step through the mnemonic one line at a time and watch which block of the ladder it builds.

L2.6Lab exercise 1 in CX-Programmer

In one sentenceIn CX-Programmer you can type the mnemonic code instead of drawing: switch to the mnemonic view, type each line, and CX-Programmer draws the ladder for you.

Program the PLC with the mnemonic code below. Type it into the editor (one instruction per line), press Compile to see the ladder, then Run it and test it with the switches. The steps to go online and transfer are the same as in Lab 1.

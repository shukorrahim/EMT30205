EMT30205 Monitoring System Integration

Interactive lecture notes · Bachelor of Electrical Maintenance System · Faculty of Electrical Engineering (FKTE)

This course is about how engineers watch and control equipment from far away: sensors, PLCs and RTUs, communication links, and the SCADA screens operators use every day.

  • Read: each chapter is one page of notes in plain English, with analogies and real Malaysian examples.
  • Try: every section has something to click, switch or simulate.
  • Check: games, quizzes and a jargon buster help you check what you have learned.

      How to use these notes

      1. 1. Read with the slidesEach page follows the lecture slides. Read it before class, or after if something was unclear.
      2. 2. Play with the screensThe dark screens copy a real SCADA HMI. Breaking things in a simulator is free; breaking them at a substation is not.
      3. 3. Check yourselfChapters 1 and 2 end with a quiz, and every chapter has games and a jargon buster. Your best quiz score is kept on this device, so you can try again before the test.
      Progress stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you switch phone or browser, you start fresh.

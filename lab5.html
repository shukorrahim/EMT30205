Counter and TimerCounting events, not seconds

L5.1What is a counter?

In one sentenceA counter captures and stores the number of times something happens: every OFF → ON change on its count line adds one, and its reset line sets it back to zero.
Think of itThe clicker a guard uses at a stadium gate. Click once for every person who walks in. When it reaches 5,000 the gate closes. At the end of the day the guard presses reset and it goes back to 0.
(Seperti alat pembilang klik yang digunakan pengawal di pintu stadium. Klik sekali bagi setiap orang yang masuk. Apabila mencecah 5,000 pintu ditutup. Pada akhir hari pengawal menekan butang set semula dan ia kembali kepada 0.)

Timer or counter?

Counters are like timers, but a timer counts time from its own internal clock, while a counter counts events from outside (a sensor, a switch) or from the program. Hold the button, let go, tap it quickly: watch both.

Counts how long the input is ON.

Counter

Counts how many times the input turns ON.

L5.3Up, down and up/down counters

In one sentenceAn up counter adds 1 for every pulse until it reaches the preset; a down counter takes 1 away for every pulse until it reaches zero; an up/down counter does both.

Most counters in industry are up counters, but some jobs need a down counter or an up/down counter (for example, cars entering and leaving a car park). PLC counters are normally retentive: after a power cut they still remember their count. Try the Power cut button.

L5.4The OMRON CNT instruction

In one sentenceCNT N #SV has two inputs: the count input (CP) and the reset input (R). Each OFF → ON pulse on CP takes 1 off the present value (PV); at 0 the counter's contact CNT N turns ON.

OMRON's CNT is a down counter: it starts at the set value (SV) and counts down to 0. Here CNT 000 #0005 lights lamp 01000 after 5 products. Press COUNT on the kit (each press is one product). Hold it down and see that holding counts only once. Then try RESET and POWER.

Mnemonic order matters: load the count input first, then the reset input, then CNT. LD 00000 (CP) · LD 00001 (R) · CNT 000 #0005. In CX-Programmer, the reset contact sits on its own line under the count contact, both joined to the CNT box. The counter's contact is written CNT000 (some models show C000).

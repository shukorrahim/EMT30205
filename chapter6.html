6.1What is IoT?

In one sentenceIoT is when everyday objects become smart by connecting to the internet to send, receive and act on data.

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices that each have four ingredients built in. Together they collect information, communicate, and respond to conditions automatically, without a person having to step in.

Analogy: giving an object senses, a brain, hands and a phoneAn ordinary fan is "dumb". Give it a thermometer (senses), a small chip that decides (a brain), a motor it can switch itself (hands) and Wi-Fi (a phone to call you), and it becomes a smart fan that turns itself on when the room is hot and tells you about it. (Analogi: memberi objek deria, otak, tangan dan telefon. Kipas biasa adalah "bodoh". Berikan ia termometer (deria), cip kecil yang membuat keputusan (otak), motor yang boleh dihidupkan sendiri (tangan) dan Wi-Fi (telefon untuk menghubungi anda), maka ia menjadi kipas pintar yang hidup sendiri apabila bilik panas dan memberitahu anda.)

6.4Why IoT in monitoring systems?

In one sentenceIoT makes monitoring more efficient, more accurate and more automatic. The notes list five benefits; try each one.
Analogy: a security guard who never sleepsWithout IoT, someone must walk around and check every machine, like a guard doing rounds every few hours. With IoT, every machine reports by itself all the time, and only phones the guard when something is wrong. (Analogi: pengawal keselamatan yang tidak pernah tidur. Tanpa IoT, seseorang perlu berjalan dan memeriksa setiap mesin, seperti pengawal yang membuat rondaan setiap beberapa jam. Dengan IoT, setiap mesin melapor sendiri sepanjang masa, dan hanya menelefon pengawal apabila ada masalah.)

      6.7IoT vs IIoT

      In one sentenceIIoT (the Industrial Internet of Things) is IoT used in plants, power grids and factories, where a failure can stop production or hurt someone, so it must be more reliable and more secure.
      Analogy: a home kitchen and a hospital kitchenBoth cook food. At home, if dinner is 20 minutes late, nobody is hurt. In a hospital kitchen, meals must be on time, clean and exactly right for every patient, every day. IoT is the home kitchen; IIoT is the hospital kitchen. (Analogi: dapur rumah dan dapur hospital. Kedua-duanya memasak makanan. Di rumah, jika makan malam lewat 20 minit, tiada siapa yang tercedera. Di dapur hospital, makanan mesti tepat pada masanya, bersih dan betul untuk setiap pesakit, setiap hari. IoT ialah dapur rumah; IIoT ialah dapur hospital.)
      AspectIoT (consumer)IIoT (industrial)

      IIoT does not replace SCADA (Chapter 1). It adds to it: SCADA keeps controlling the plant in real time, while IIoT sensors and cloud analytics watch machine health, energy and trends.
